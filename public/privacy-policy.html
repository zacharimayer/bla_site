Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

The Broward Leadership Alliance ("BLA", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information when you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact form or other interactions:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message content
  • SMS consent preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you
  • To send campaign text messages if you have opted in
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Text Message (SMS) Communications

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages from Eugen Bold for Broward County Commission, District 6, you agree to the following terms:

  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Message frequency varies
  • Reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time
  • Reply HELP for assistance
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase or participation

We will not share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and security measures. We use Supabase, a secure cloud database platform, to store form submissions. While we implement reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties except when necessary to comply with law, enforce our site policies, or protect our or others' rights, property, or safety.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for SMS messaging by texting STOP

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:
